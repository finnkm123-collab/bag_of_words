Association football, commonly known as football or soccer, is a team sport played between two teams of 11 players who mostly use their feet to kick an inflated ball around a pitch (playing field).

The objective of the game is to score more goals than the opposing team by moving the ball into the rectangular-framed goal which that team defends. 
Traditionally, the game has been played over two 45-minute halves, for a total match time of 90 minutes. 
With an estimated 250 million players active in over 200 countries and territories, it is the world's most popular sport.
